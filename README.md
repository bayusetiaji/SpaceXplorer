# SpaceXplorer

A simple *shoot 'em all* endless shooting game. Dodge the asteroid belt, blast rocks for points, and keep an eye on your shield, fuel and ammo.

## Story
A spaceship explores the sky and meets many asteroids. The ship has to avoid each asteroid or shoot them to get score. If the ship hits an asteroid, it will decrease health points. The ship can recover by hitting an aid kit canister. During flight the ship also has to refill fuel by hitting a fuel can.

## Controls

| Action | Mouse / Keyboard | Gamepad / Touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move | Move the mouse, or `A` / `D` (`←` / `→`) | Left stick / drag on screen |
| Fire | Hold left click, or hold `Space` / `↑` | `A` / hold touch |
| Pause | `P` or `Esc` | Start |
| Mute | `M` | — |
| Fullscreen | `F11` or `Alt` + `Enter` | — |

The ship auto-fires while the button is held. On the title screen, press `Space`, click, or tap anywhere to launch.

## Tips
- A shield hit grants a short invulnerability window — use it to slip away.
- Damaged shield? An aid kit will drift in. Low on fuel? A fuel can is on its way.
- Destroying asteroids tops up your ammo reserve.
- Your best score is saved between runs.

## Platform
To run the game you need to install the LUA interpreter and the Love2D engine from:
- [LUA download](https://www.lua.org/download.html)
- [Love2D download](https://love2d.org)

The window is resizable, and the play-field keeps its original 380×640 proportions at any size.
